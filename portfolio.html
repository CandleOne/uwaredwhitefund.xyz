Portfolio Overview

Investment Strategy

The Red & White Fund follows a disciplined, long-term approach to portfolio management. Student analysts research opportunities, evaluate risk, and present recommendations for review.

Portfolio decisions are guided by diversification, stewardship, and the Fund's commitment to creating a practical learning environment for student investment managers.

The investment objective of the Red & White Fund is to pursue sustainable long-run returns while meeting its risk and stewardship commitments.

The Fund's risk tolerance and investment guidelines are maintained in its Investment Policy Statement.
