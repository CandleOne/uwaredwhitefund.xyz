University algorithmic trading clubs

Student organizations focused on algorithmic and quantitative trading.

United States

  • Iowa State University: Algorithmic Trading ClubWorkshops, guest lectures, a trading competition, and quant firm visits.
  • University of Washington: Algorithmic Trading ClubFounded 2019; Python, backtesting, and deploying trading algorithms.
  • UCLA: Bruin Algorithmic TradersNewer club; strategy training and paper trading through Interactive Brokers.

United Kingdom

  • Imperial College London: Algorithmic Trading Society (AlgoSoc)Hosts the Algothon hackathon; linked to the student-managed fund Queen's Tower Capital.
  • University of Cambridge: Algorithmic Trading Society (CUATS)Coding sessions, workshops, competitions, and speaker events.
