University trading and student-managed investment clubs

Student clubs that trade or manage real portfolios, starting with Alabama and neighboring states.

Alabama

  • University of Alabama: Culverhouse Investment Management GroupStudent-run fund managing a portfolio of over $2.2 million; members have landed roles at firms including Jane Street and Point72.
  • Samford University: Bulldog Investment FundLaunched in 2008 as a course; described as the largest student-managed fund in Alabama.

Adjacent states

  • Florida State University: Student Investment FundAbout $6.5 million in assets as of March 2024; managers study trading strategies, derivatives, and arbitrage.
  • University of South Florida: Student Managed Investment FundAnnual reports and student analyst reports from a fund inside the USF Foundation portfolio.
  • University of North Georgia: Student Investment ClubManages a fund seeded with $50,000 from alumni; uses Bloomberg Terminals.
  • Southern Adventist University (Tennessee): SMARRT FundStudents manage $500,000, about 1% of the university's endowment.

Elsewhere in the United States

  • Fairleigh Dickinson University: Student Managed Investment FundLaunched in 2022 with $100,000 in seed money; open to students of any major.

United Kingdom

  • Queen's University Belfast: Trading and Investment Club and Student Managed FundLaunched in 2012 around a trading room; includes simulated trading and competitions.
  • Queen Mary University of London: MSc Investment Fund (QUMMIF)Postgraduate student-managed fund with over £40,000 under management.
