Scholarship

Scholarship Funding

The Red & White Fund targets an annual distribution of 5% for student scholarships and programs, supplemented by funding from outside benefactors. Distributions are generated through a total-return approach: interest, dividends, and capital appreciation.



How Funds Are Distributed

The ACFN Department Chair prioritizes the distribution of funds based on the needs of student programs and scholarships. Restricted funds are maintained and distributed according to their stated purpose.



Available Scholarships

  • Top Performer Scholarship: awarded to the top-performing members of the Fund.
  • Most Technically Impressive Investment Strategy Scholarship: awarded for the most technically impressive investment strategy developed within the Fund.
  • Top Portfolio Manager (Math Major) Scholarship: awarded to the top-performing portfolio manager majoring in mathematics.
